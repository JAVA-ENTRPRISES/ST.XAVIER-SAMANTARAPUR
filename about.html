Who We Are

St. Xavier High School, Samantarapur, Bhubaneswar, is a premier co-educational English-medium institution affiliated with CBSE. Our school is committed to delivering quality education while nurturing creativity, discipline, and leadership among students.

🎯 Our Vision

To create responsible global citizens who excel in academics, innovation, and moral values.

🚀 Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive, and stimulating environment where every child can achieve their full potential.
